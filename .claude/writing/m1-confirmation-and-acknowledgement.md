# M1 — confirmation and acknowledgement

- Source: https://m1.material.io/patterns/confirmation-acknowledgement.html
- Captured: 2026-08-13
- Older Material site; M3 has no equivalent page. Condensed in our own words, not a copy.

## The two things
- **Confirmation** — ask the user to verify that they really want the action they just started. It
  can carry a warning or the critical detail they need first.
- **Acknowledgement** — tell the user the action they chose is done.

## When to confirm
Do not confirm when the consequences are reversible or trivial. Save it for what the user cannot
easily undo.

## Wording
A confirmation dialog's title must be meaningful and echo the action that was requested.

The page gives no guidance on button labels — no standard set of verbs is defined here.
