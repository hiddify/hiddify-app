# M3 — word choice

- Source: https://m3.material.io/foundations/content-design/style-guide/word-choice
- Captured: 2026-08-13
- Scope: English UX text. The page covers pronouns only. Condensed in our own words, not a copy.

## Second person
Write "you" and "your". It makes the interface sound like it is speaking to the user about their own
actions, instead of sounding impersonal and mechanical.

## Never mix first and second person
"me" and "my" next to "you" and "your" confuses the reader. Pick "you"/"your" for the whole screen,
or drop the pronoun.

## Careful with "I" and "we"
Written for a large company, "we" and "I" sound robotic or unsettling. Stay on the user's side of
the sentence and try rewriting without "we" at all. This holds even when a real person is behind the
words — a first-person pronoun in UI text still reads as jarring.

Legal text is the exception: "I" and "my" mark ownership of a decision, as in "I agree to the terms
of service." Use it where the user is committing to something that matters.
