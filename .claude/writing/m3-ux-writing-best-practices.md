# M3 — UX writing best practices

- Source: https://m3.material.io/foundations/content-design/style-guide/ux-writing-best-practices
- Captured: 2026-08-13
- Scope: English UX text. Condensed in our own words, not a copy of the page.

## Explain consequences
Say what an action will do, in plain neutral words, and how to undo it.
Do not dress it up as a warning — alarming, intimidating or condescending text pushes the user
around. Do not misstate the outcome to steer the decision either.

## Write so it can be scanned
People skim. Titles and headings must name their topic exactly, so someone in a hurry lands on the
right thing and does not act by mistake. Group and rank information with headings and subheads.

## Sentence case
Only the first word takes a capital — in every string: titles, headings, labels, menu items,
navigation, app bars, buttons. Product and brand names keep their own capitals.
Never title case.

## Few abbreviations
Spell the word out when there is room. Short forms are harder to read and screen readers handle them
badly. No Latin abbreviations in UI text — write "for example" and "and more", not "e.g." and "etc."
When an abbreviation is the right call, get its spelling and format right.
