# M1 — empty states

- Source: https://m1.material.io/patterns/empty-states.html
- Captured: 2026-08-13
- Older Material site; M3 has no equivalent page. Condensed in our own words, not a copy.

## The tagline
- Positive in tone, and in keeping with the product's voice.
- Say what the screen is for, without sounding like a button. Wording that reads as a call to action
  makes people tap the text or the image expecting something to happen.

## The image
Non-interactive, quiet against the background, and it should convey what the screen is for in a
lively way — the app icon works.

## Better than an empty screen
1. **Starter content** — sample material the user can explore instead of a blank list.
2. **Educational content** — a dismissible card explaining what will appear here once there is
   content.
3. **Best match** — for a misspelled query, show close results, clearly labelled so they are not
   mistaken for real matches.

The page does not distinguish a first-use empty state from one the user emptied, or from an error.
