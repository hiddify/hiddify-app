# Woman in Red App Icon Design

## Goal

Create an original, recognizable app-icon master for Woman in Red that remains legible at small iOS and Android icon sizes.

## Visual Design

- Use a single centered silhouette of an elegant floor-length red evening dress.
- Suggest a poised femme-fatale character through the dress shape only; do not depict or copy an existing character.
- Render the dress in the brand accent `#FF2D3E` on the near-black `#090A0F` background.
- Place sparse, subtle Matrix-inspired digital glyphs inside the red fabric. The glyphs are texture, not the main subject.
- Keep the outer silhouette bold, smooth, and vector-friendly with generous safe margins for platform icon cropping.

## Constraints

- Square 1:1 composition, suitable as a 1024 x 1024 app-icon master.
- No text, letters, wordmark, face, body details, device mockup, border, or watermark.
- No direct resemblance to Hiddify branding or any copyrighted character.
- No dense code rain, thin outlines, blue or purple accents, glossy 3D effects, or generic shield/padlock imagery.
- The dress must remain recognizable when reduced to 32 x 32 pixels.

## Acceptance Check

The result succeeds when the first read is "red dress," the second read is "digital privacy," and the mark remains clean and distinctive at app-icon scale.
